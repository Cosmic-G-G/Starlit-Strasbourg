In this gamemode, the gang is being chased on vehicle by pursuers and needs to shake them off. Each character on the team has a unique role and combat mechanic in this gamemode:

Matt
: Responsible for driving the vehicle. While the player is in control of Matt, the camera is probably best from a third person perspective. When not in control (autopilot) the camera faces the vehicle from a second person perspective to help you aim at the enemies behind. 

Shark
: Can leap onto enemy vehicles to perform assassinations, ripping the driver out of the driver seat causing the enemy vehicle to crash. Shark can also use her spear to puncture the enemy engine, then leap away while it swerves and crashes. Undecided if the camera should follow Shark.

Ellie
: Ellie pops up through the vehicle's sun roof or leans out the passenger side to fire her arm cannon or rocket launcher which blows up enemy vehicles. Can affect adjacent vehicles. 
> Scene idea. Vehicle emerges from the explosion plume. Can be the player vehicle, or the enemy vehicle.

Barcelona
: Leans out of the passenger side / shotgun seat to systematically pinpoint key enemies shooting at you, pop tires, or target drivers. 

Visor
: Same as Barcelona.

Notes:
- The characters maybe have a cooldown for each of their attacks, so one character can't dominate all the enemy pursuers.
- In multiplayer setting, you control a specific character. Generally, the screen for both players is the same (second person), except if one of the players is Matt
