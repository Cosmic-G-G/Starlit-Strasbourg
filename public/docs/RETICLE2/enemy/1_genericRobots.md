In this first arc of the game, the team fights against rogue robotic lifeforms. Some are vagabonds, others are organized militia. Other arcs will fight human factions, alien factions or other factions relevant to the world lore. 

Here are some robotic enemies:
Tau
: A generic robot soldier, this model was designed to be tankier and withstand general ballistics. 

Pi
: A robotic sharpshooter. This model exceeds at long range, but is quite fragile.

Theta
: Your generic robot footsoldier. A few good shots to the dome puts this model to rest.
