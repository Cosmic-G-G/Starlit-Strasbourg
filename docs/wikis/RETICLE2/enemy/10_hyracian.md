Hyracians are an alien form that has a high integration with computers and technology, so much so that they have digitalized and mechanized parts of themselves. They also mass produce robot soldiers and often use those robots as combat units/usually on the same team.

But the Hyracians are so good at mechanics that some of the robots they made developed sentience and independence. So the robots are sort of a faction on their own, but commonly associated with the Hyracians.

The Hyracians as a race are extremely learned and very advanced. This is due to the alien forms evolving a complex hivemind that affects nearly every individual. It is extremely difficult to find individual Hyracians in day to day since they typically do not settle in non-Hyracian cities except for infiltration.

Ellie is important because she was a Hyracian exhibiting a connection but no influence from the hivemind, and is therefore an extreme threat to the Hyracians.
