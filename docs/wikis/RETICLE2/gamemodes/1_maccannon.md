There is one gamemode where the squad has to defend a Confederate Outpost from airborne Hyracian battle ships. But the auto defenses are down, so the squad members need to manually mount the cannons to shoot the invaders out of the sky.

These controls are similar to regular turrets. In this level, there are multiple turrets for each character and each of the turrets has a different effect.

Sniper turret
: Shoots a single projectile streak which pierces ships and deals high damage. Weak ships explode on contact, and strong ships only take a few shots to kill

Tesla turret
: Shoots a beam of lightning that jumps to other nearby ships. Weak ships are destroyed, strong ships are stunned.

Mortar turret
: Shoots a projectile in an arc, so that the projectil comes from the top. Deals piercing damage and explodes.

One member should clear the research outpost of enemies and reactivate auto defenses. On doing this, the turrets enter their own overclock mode for the final boss of the stage. After dismounting the turrets and defeating the boss, the turrets remain in overclock mode.
