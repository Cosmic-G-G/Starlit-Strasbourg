It's dusk, but an eclipse shy of the planets horizon creates a surreal junction between dusk and dawn, a thin line of light right at the horizons edge. Escape pods descend from the sky, the last hope of a people streaking through the dark blue. Is it a civilian ship? A research ship?


Remember the image, it won't be the last time you'll see it. But right now, you're not here to ask questions. Turn up the radio on your jeep 52' hundred and pump the gas. Their pursuers won't be far behind and you've got a ways to go driving through this desolate Arizona desert. Your destination lays ahead, the pod's impact zone: a post-modern futuristic metropolis shining like silverware on the horizon's edge.


When Matt and Shark arrive at the impact zone, they find that the enemies have already arrived first. Essentially night time, debris dust and asphalt covers the city, laid to waste by the Hyracians and robots. 


The miasma in the area is bad. Matt wears a gas mask and Shark mask is integrated onto her face. More than just masks, these specialized pieces of equipment are confidential confederate technology, sustaining crucial life support functions and capable of activating powerful armor abilities called 'overclock' features. 


Here, you meet the payload of the operation. Administration puts on a facade, claiming a charitable rescue operation. You're not convinced. If so, why the secrecy, why do we need to drive into the city from so far away instead of being dropped closer to the action? You surmise that it might be some bigwig or researcher, and even your intercom dispatcher is not privy to the details. But when you meet the payload, it is neither a bigwig nor a researcher. Instead, it is young girl, and to your surprise, a Hyracian. The very enemy you are meant to destroy. 


The girl has glowing blue veins like circuitry across her skin and face. Her arm is hidden, probably due to recent injuries she sustained during recent events.  Matt sees that the Hyracian girl doesn't have a gas mask and provides her own.
