The story is presented through environment and dialogue. Generally it's ok to just throw the player into the mission without context of where you are or why you're here, as that can be presented through dialogue over the duration of the level.

Aim for short meaningful dialogue interactions, although not always possible. Dialogue mainly occurs during gameplay, such as through the intercom or through npc dialogue. It is important that the player is not sitting and listening to dialogue without actively doing stuff at the same time. Exception is cutscenes because they're cool.
