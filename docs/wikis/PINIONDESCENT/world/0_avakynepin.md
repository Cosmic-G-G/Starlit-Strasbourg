Avakyne's arsenal of swords and spears are called 'Heavenly Pins' by the mortals.  

Just like pins in real life, they can be used to 'pin' down something to the ground. Similarly, Avakyne's pins are cast down into the earth, triggering the beginning of the apocalypse. When this happens, the pins aren't swords, but rather hugh skyscraping towers that pulsate with divine energy.

One particular pin in this story was usurped by one of the gods of eons past. This sword (which is actually a sword not a skyscraper) is called the World Cleaver. Its special ability, World Rend, strikes through the air with a resonating toll, and splits the timeline into multiple AUs.

After the main character defeats the god and receives the pin, he uses it to create a timeline where he recovers all his former strength. Disappointingly, as soon as he uses it, it shatters and disintegrates into dust. However, there is more than meets the eye.

When the main character used World Rend, he created an alternate timeline where he recovered all his power. It is an apocalyptic world where he has become a corrupt god, and caused many of his companions to suffer and turn against him. In his regret, he uses World Rend to destroy the sword in the original timeline, which is why it disintegrated in the main timeline. 

Worse yet, his use of World Rend alerted the one Immaculate he should not have, Avakyne, who now descends upon the apocalyptic world to reclaim her pin and bring destined destruction.

Because of all the chaotic energy in the apocalypse timeline, the main character of the original timeline sometimes gets pulled in by the chaotic gravity, where he discovers the cost of power, and is tasked with rectifying his mistakes and saving the lives he can.
